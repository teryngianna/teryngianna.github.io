about me

Everything I create is a reflection of who I am.

I’m Ma. Teryn Gianna L. Nuza, also known as Aria. I’m a 4th-year Multimedia Arts student at National University Laguna and a part-time content creator.

My passion is turning ideas and emotions into stories across many mediums. My art is where I explore emotions, beauty, and stories that matter, often blending softness and playfulness with deeper themes like mental health, identity, and human connection.

My goal is to leave a mark on this world through my creations: work that captures attention, sparks emotion, and inspires others.

Athens’ Dollhouse

Digital illustration, made in Ibis Paint X

A digital illustration inspired by my friend, Athens Gutierrez, who shares my love for femininity, pink, dolls, and coquette-inspired aesthetics.

Inspired by vintage dolls, pastel colors, ribbons, roses, and romantic details, I imagined Athens as a character in her own little dollhouse. The piece explores femininity, nostalgia, and the parts of childhood we carry with us as we grow older.

The reference

I started from Athens’ own photo, then pushed it toward a softer, doll-like look with Douyin-inspired makeup, lace textures, and glitter.

Photo by Athens Gutierrez, @babydollscakee
